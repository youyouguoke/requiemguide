Emily is a key character Grace encounters inside the Rhodes Hill Care Center **isolation ward**. She is **blind** — a result of human experimentation — and is in fact **a clone created by The Connections**, designated **Subject 171**. Her ability to read **Braille** makes her essential to the [Star Quartz puzzle](/puzzles/star-quartz-box).

## Background

Grace takes responsibility for protecting Emily, and their bond becomes one of the driving forces of the narrative. Emily cannot defend herself; keeping her close is both a mechanical and emotional thread through the Care Center.

## Key Traits

- **Role**: Survivor, puzzle helper, emotional core
- **Ability**: Reads the Braille on the Star Quartz puzzle box
- **Vulnerability**: Cannot defend herself; must be protected by Grace

## Story Role

Emily's injury during the helicopter escape is a **scripted story beat**, not a consequence of player choices. She mutates and attacks Grace, and Leon steps in. The [Emily (Transformed) encounter](/bosses/emily-transformed) and the [endings guide](/story/endings) cover what follows — including the true ending, where Elpis cures Emily and Grace adopts her.
