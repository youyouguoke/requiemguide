Grace Ashcroft is the primary protagonist of **Resident Evil Requiem** — an **FBI intelligence analyst** who investigates the Rhodes Hill Care Center, the same facility where her mother **Alyssa Ashcroft** (a survivor of the Raccoon City outbreak) was murdered. Later in the story, Grace learns she was adopted by **Oswell E. Spencer**.

## Background

Grace's campaign is classic survival horror in first person: stealth, light management, and puzzle-solving rather than direct confrontation. She relies on her flashlight and wits — and on Emily, the blind girl she rescues from the isolation ward, who can read the Braille needed for the [Star Quartz puzzle](/puzzles/star-quartz-box).

## Key Traits

- **Playstyle**: First-person stealth, light manipulation, puzzle-solving
- **Signature Threat**: The Girl ("Shadow Ghost")
- **Weapon Affinity**: Handguns, especially the S&S M232
- **Strengths**: Quiet movement, environmental awareness, puzzle intuition

## Story Role

Grace carries the emotional core of the game: her search for answers about her mother's death, her bond with Emily, and her partnership with Leon. At the ARK she makes the game's final choice about Elpis — release or destroy — which decides the ending. See the [endings guide](/story/endings) for what each choice means.

## Notable Equipment

- **B934**: Starting handgun
- **S&S M232**: Superior handgun recovered from the Bar & Lounge, West Wing 2F
- **Requiem**: 12.7x55mm magnum revolver Leon hands to Grace during the story
