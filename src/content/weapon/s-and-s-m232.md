The **S&S M232** is widely considered the best handgun for Grace in *Resident Evil Requiem*. It outperforms the starting B934 in every category and allows consistent headshot staggers once the stabilizer upgrade is installed. This guide covers the exact S&S M232 location, full stats, damage values, best upgrades, and how it compares to the B934 and Requiem magnum.

## Why the S&S M232 Is Grace's Best Handgun

Grace starts with the B934, but the M232 is a direct replacement that makes every common encounter easier. Its combination of stability, precision, and common 9mm ammo means it can serve as your primary weapon for the rest of the campaign. The key is finding it early in Chapter 3 and installing the stabilizer upgrade as soon as possible.

## Where to Find the S&S M232

The S&S M232 is located in the **Care Center Bar and Lounge** in the West Wing. You reach this area naturally during Chapter 3 while searching for Quartz pieces.

### Step-by-Step Location

1. Progress through the West Wing until you reach the Care Center.
2. Enter the Bar and Lounge after the broken elevator hallway.
3. Look for the wooden counter near the cash register.
4. The M232 sits on the counter and can be picked up permanently.

This pickup is not missable. The counter is directly along the main path, and you will pass it during the Quartz search sequence.

The M232's stats are balanced for sustained medium-range combat. It does not hit as hard as the Requiem magnum, but its stability lets you land multiple headshots quickly without losing control.

## Best Upgrades for the S&S M232

Upgrade priority changes how the M232 feels. Follow this order:

1. **Stabilizers** — The most important upgrade. A single weak-point shot staggers most common enemies, opening them up for melee follow-ups.
2. **Capacity** — Increases how many shots you can fire before reloading, which matters in rooms with multiple enemies.
3. **Power** — Useful for mini-bosses and higher difficulties, but only after the first two upgrades.

| Feature | B934 | S&S M232 |

| Damage | Low-Medium | Medium |

The B934 is serviceable in the opening chapters, but the M232 is better in every meaningful way. Switch as soon as you find it and retire the B934 to backup status.

## Best Bosses to Use the S&S M232 Against

### Shadow Ghost

The Shadow Ghost is vulnerable after being stunned by light. The M232's precision lets you pump shots into its weak point during these windows without wasting ammo.

### Chunk

While Leon is the primary character for the Chunk fight, Grace faces similar mini-boss encounters where the M232's stagger helps control smaller enemies while you chip away at the boss.

### Blister Borne

The M232 is useful for popping Blister Borne cysts from mid-range before switching to the Requiem magnum for the main health bar.

## Tips for Using the S&S M232

- Install Stabilizers first. A single weak-point shot will stagger most common enemies after this upgrade.
- Use the M232 as your main weapon for the rest of Grace's campaign.
- Save 9mm ammo for the M232 rather than wasting it on the B934 after you switch.
- Aim for the head or glowing cysts to trigger staggers and melee follow-ups.
- Pair with the Requiem magnum for bosses: M232 for adds, Requiem for the boss health bar.
