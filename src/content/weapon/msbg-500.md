The **MSBG 500** is Leon's pump-action shotgun in *Resident Evil Requiem*, found in the **Care Center Attic** along the main path before the [Chunk](/bosses/chunk) fight. It remains a staple of Leon's combat-heavy sections throughout the game.

## Why the MSBG 500 Matters for Leon

Leon's campaign leans into action sequences with multiple enemies closing in at once. The MSBG 500 is built for exactly that: a single close-range shot can kill common enemies outright, and well-placed shots can stagger bosses long enough to reload or reposition. Its weakness is range — pair it with a handgun for anything at distance.

## Where to Find the MSBG 500

The MSBG 500 is in the **Care Center Attic**, shortly before Leon's fight with Chunk.

### Step-by-Step Location

1. Progress through the Care Center with Leon until you reach the Attic.
2. Follow the main path through the Attic — the shotgun sits next to a box of shotgun shells.
3. Pick it up before dropping into the Chunk encounter.

## Upgrades

The MSBG 500 is upgraded through the **Supply Box**. Prioritize **capacity** first — the base tube is small, and reloading mid-fight leaves Leon vulnerable — then fire rate and power for boss damage.

## Best Bosses to Use the MSBG 500 Against

### Chunk

The Chunk fight happens right after you pick up the MSBG 500. Stagger him after he attacks, then unload into his head while he recovers.

### Blister Borne

When Blister Borne gets close, the shotgun's spread lets you pressure its orange cysts while staggering the boss — see the [Blister Borne guide](/bosses/blister-borne) for the full pattern.
