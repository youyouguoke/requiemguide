The **Kotetsu** is a post-game melee weapon in *Resident Evil Requiem* that never breaks. It is an excellent choice for replaying the game on higher difficulties or tackling challenge modes without worrying about durability.

This guide covers the Kotetsu unlock method, stats, best use cases, and how it compares to other weapons in the game.

## Kotetsu Overview

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Type | Melee |
| Damage | High |
| Light Attack | 600 per hit |
| Heavy Attack | 1,100 per hit |
| Durability | Infinite |
| Ammo | None |
| Range | Short |
| Character | Grace or Leon |

The Kotetsu is all about economy. It does not deal the highest damage in the game, but its infinite durability and zero ammo cost make it the perfect tool for replaying the game and conserving resources.

## How to Unlock the Kotetsu

The Kotetsu is **not found during the main campaign**. It is unlocked after completing the game once and starting New Game+ or challenge mode. The exact ending does not matter; any completed campaign unlocks the weapon.

### Step-by-Step Unlock

1. Finish the main campaign at least once on any difficulty.
2. Start New Game+ or challenge mode.
3. Open the item box at the first safe room.
4. The Kotetsu will be available as an inventory item.

## Best Use Cases for the Kotetsu

- **Ammo conservation on standard difficulty replays.**
- **Challenge modes where resources are limited.**
- **Close-quarters ambushes when you want to save gun ammo.**
- **New Game+ runs where you want to melee common enemies for fun.**

## Kotetsu vs Other Weapons

| Weapon | Type | Best For | Ammo Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kotetsu | Melee | Ammo conservation, challenge mode | None |
| Requiem revolver | Magnum (revolver) | Bosses, long-range precision | Rare |
| MSBG 500 | Shotgun | Crowd control, boss stagger | Uncommon |
| S&S M232 | Handgun | Standard enemies, headshot staggers | Common |

The Kotetsu does not replace ranged weapons. It is a niche tool for players who want to conserve ammo or challenge themselves with melee-only runs.

## Tips for Using the Kotetsu

- Use it to conserve ammo on standard difficulty replays.
- Pair with movement upgrades to close gaps quickly.
- Be careful against ranged or flying enemies; the Kotetsu has no projectile capability.
- It is not ideal for boss fights unless you are very confident in your melee timing.
- Heavy attacks deal the most damage but have a slower recovery window.
