Resident Evil Requiem has **five combination safes**, all in Grace's sections of the Care Center and the ARK. Combinations are **fixed**, but **Insanity difficulty uses different codes**. Opening all five unlocks the **No Safe is Safe** trophy.

## How to Read the Codes

Safes are dial locks. A code like **10L-80R-30L** means: turn to 10 (left), then 80 (right), then 30 (left).

## All Five Safes

### 1. Bar & Lounge Safe — Care Center, West Wing (behind the bar)

- **Normal:** 10L-80R-30L
- **Insanity:** 20R-50L-30R
- **Reward:** 3 Antique Coins
- **Code clue:** a file in the Office / Custodian's Office 2F

### 2. Examination Room Safe — Care Center, East Wing

- **Normal:** 30R-10L-50R
- **Insanity:** 50L-30R-90L
- **Reward:** 3 Antique Coins
- **Code clue:** near the lockers outside the Conference Room

### 3. Basement Safe — Care Center Basement

- **Normal:** 60R-40L-80R
- **Insanity:** 70L-50R-20L
- **Reward:** Raccoon Roundup Map + Handgun Ammo
- **Code clue:** only obtainable as **Leon** in the Attic warped cabinet segment

### 4. Sterilization Chamber Safe — ARK

- **Normal:** 10R-90L-20R
- **Insanity:** 80L-50R-0L
- **Reward:** Molotov + Med Injector + Handgun Ammo

### 5. Monitor Control Room Safe — ARK

- **Normal:** 50L-60R-80L
- **Insanity:** 20R-0L-70R
- **Reward:** Med Injector + Empty Bottle + Bottle of Acid
- **Code clue:** inside a Bioweapon Repository 05 shipping container

## Tips

- The Basement safe is easy to miss: its clue is in Leon's segment, so note the combination when you see it — Grace opens the safe later.
- On Insanity, write down the new combinations as you find them; every code changes.
- Antique Coins from the first two safes feed the coin-spending unlocks, so prioritize the Care Center safes early.
