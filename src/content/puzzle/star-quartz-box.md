The **Star Quartz Puzzle Box** is the last of the three Quartz puzzles. It sits in the **Security Manager's Office**, behind a Level 2 security door in the Isolation Ward area of the Care Center.

## Where to Find the Star Quartz Puzzle Box

The office requires the **ID Wristband (Level 2)**. You get it by **reviving the corpse in the East Wing Examination Room** — this is a story-adjacent interaction, not a hidden pickup.

## What You Need Before Starting

- **ID Wristband (Level 2):** from the Examination Room corpse — opens the Isolation Ward.
- **ID Wristband (Level 3):** from the Security Manager's body in his office — opens Emily's cell.
- **Emily:** the box buttons are worn smooth and the code is in **Braille** — only Emily can read it. She waits in the **Medication Room**.

## Exact Solution

1. Enter the Security Manager's Office (Isolation Ward, Level 2 door) and read the **notebook on the desk** — unfold its upper-right corner for the clue.
2. Examine the puzzle box. The buttons are worn smooth and the code is in **Braille**, which Grace cannot read — so take the **ID Wristband (Level 3)** from the **Security Manager's body** at the desk.
3. Use Level 3 clearance to open **Emily's cell** in the Medication Room and carry her back to the office.
4. Bring **Emily** to the puzzle box and have her read the Braille buttons. Defend her while she works. The sequence is:

   1. **Star**
   2. **Star**
   3. **Moon**
   4. **Sun**

5. After the **Care Center Basement rescue sequence**, Emily hands you the **Star Quartz**.

## What You Get

- **Star Quartz:** the third and final Quartz piece for the Central Hall mechanism.
- **ID Wristband (Level 3):** taken from the Security Manager's body in the same office — it opens Emily's cell and other red security doors.

## What To Do Next

With all three Quartz pieces, return to the [Central Hall Quartz Mechanism](/puzzles/central-hall-quartz) on the 1st floor to open the way to the Courtyard.

## Common Mistakes

- Searching for the Level 2 Wristband in lockers — it comes from the Examination Room corpse.
- Trying to enter the code yourself — the Braille buttons cannot be used without Emily.
- Forgetting the notebook on the Security Manager's desk.
