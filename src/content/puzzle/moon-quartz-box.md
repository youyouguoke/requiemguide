The **Moon Quartz Puzzle Box** is the first Quartz puzzle needed to unlock the Central Hall door. It is found in the **Chairman's Office** on the second floor of the West Wing.

This guide covers the exact location, the Pencil item, the symbol sequence, and how to use the Moon Quartz to progress.

## Where to Find the Moon Quartz Puzzle Box

The puzzle box is located on a small cabinet in the **Chairman's Office**, West Wing 2F. The office is accessible once Grace can explore the West Wing. The room is not guarded, but the hallway outside may have infected staff — clear the area before settling in to solve the box.

## Required Item: The Pencil

The **Pencil** is on the same desk as the torn notebook. It is impossible to miss if you thoroughly search the office. To reveal the code:

1. Pick up the **Pencil** from the desk.
2. Examine the **torn notebook** in your inventory.
3. Use the Pencil on the notebook to reveal the symbol sequence: **Moon → Sun → Star → Moon**.

## Moon Quartz Box Solution

Input the sequence on the puzzle box in the order shown on the notebook:

1. **Moon**
2. **Sun**
3. **Star**
4. **Moon**

The box will open and you will receive the **Moon Quartz**, the first of three Quartz pieces needed for the Central Hall mechanism.

## Reward

You receive the **Moon Quartz**, one of three required pieces for the Central Hall door. Without this item, you cannot proceed to the courtyard.

## Tips

- Search the office thoroughly before leaving; there are files and supplies worth grabbing.
- If you forget the sequence, check your inventory notes or return to the notebook.
- The box itself is not guarded, but the West Wing has more enemies than the East Wing.
- Pick up the Pencil as soon as you see it; it is required for the puzzle.

## Common Mistakes

- Leaving the office without the Pencil and having to backtrack.
- Forgetting the exact sequence. The notebook remains in your inventory, so you can recheck it.
- Trying to open the box without examining the notebook first.

## Next Steps

After collecting the Moon Quartz, head to the **Lead Researcher's Office** on the East Wing 2F to solve the **Sun Quartz Puzzle Box**. You will need the **Corrosive** from the Conference Room first.
