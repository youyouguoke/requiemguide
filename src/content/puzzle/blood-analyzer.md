The **Blood Analyzer** puzzles are 3×3 atom-grid terminals in the Care Center. Clicking a node toggles the atoms; turn them all red to solve. There are **three specimens on Normal/Standard difficulties** and a **fourth, Insanity-only** one. Solving them unlocks crafting recipes — including a permanent health upgrade.

## Where to Find the Analyzers

- **Main analyzer:** Blood Lab, Care Center **East Wing 1F**
- **Second analyzer:** **West Wing Office, 2nd floor**

## All Solutions

### Denatured Blood — Blood Lab

- **Solution:** click the **left-middle** node once.
- **Reward:** Handgun Ammo + **Hemolytic Injector** recipe

### Reversible Blood — room next to the Blood Lab

- **Requirement:** **ID Wristband Lv1**
- **Solution:** **bottom-left → middle → top-right → 2nd from top**
- **Reward:** **Steroids** + Stabilizer

### Converged Blood — closet south of the Waiting Room

- **Requirement:** **ID Wristband Lv1** (on Insanity this room is the Records Room)
- **Solution:** **top-middle → 2nd square from bottom**
- **Reward:** Med Injector + **12.7x55mm** ammo

### Polymerized Blood — Insanity only

- **Location:** same closet as Converged, appears only on Insanity difficulty
- **Reward:** **R.I.P. Knife** + Handgun Ammo x30

## Why These Puzzles Matter

The **Steroids** reward from Reversible Blood is a **permanent maximum health increase** — one of the best upgrades in the game, especially on higher difficulties. The Hemolytic Injector recipe adds a strong craftable healing item.

## Common Mistakes

- Clicking the center node on Denatured Blood — the correct single click is the **left-middle** node.
- Skipping the Waiting Room closet and missing Converged Blood entirely.
- Forgetting that the fourth specimen only appears on Insanity.
