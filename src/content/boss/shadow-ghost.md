The **Shadow Ghost** — officially called **The Girl** — is a recurring stalker in *Resident Evil Requiem*. She haunts the dark corridors of the Rhodes Hill Care Center and reacts aggressively to sound and movement. She cannot be killed with ordinary weapons; light is your only real defense, and the main battle takes place at the Water Treatment Plant late in Grace's campaign.

This guide covers the Shadow Ghost location, weakness, recommended loadout, attack patterns, phase strategy, and tips for beating it on Standard and Hardcore difficulty.

## Shadow Ghost Overview

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Location | Water Treatment Plant, Rhodes Hill Care Center |
| Chapter | 3 |
| Difficulty | Medium |
| HP | Cannot be killed directly; repelled by light |
| Weakness | Light sources (flashlight, flares, lamps) |
| Resists | Dark environments, raw gun damage without light |
| Recommended Weapon | Flashlight + S&S M232 handgun |
| Reward | Exit from Water Treatment Plant |

The Shadow Ghost is a unique boss because it is not defeated by raw damage. Instead, Grace must manage light sources and use the brief stagger windows to deal damage. This makes it a resource-management fight rather than a DPS race.

## Where to Find the Shadow Ghost

The main Shadow Ghost battle is triggered at the Water Treatment Plant late in Grace's campaign. Before that, the ghost appears in several dark corridors as a stalking enemy that cannot be killed. The final battle ends when the ghost retreats and the exit unlocks.

## Best Loadout for the Shadow Ghost

Before the fight, make sure you have:

- **Flashlight with full battery** — The most important tool
- **S&S M232 handgun** — For stagger windows
- **Handgun Ammo** — only needed for short stagger windows
- **Flares x 2-3** — Create safe zones in large rooms
- **Green Herbs x 2** — For unavoidable damage

## Phase-by-Phase Strategy

### Phase 1: Stalk (Early Encounters)

The Shadow Ghost appears in dark corridors and reacts to sound and movement. Walk slowly, keep the flashlight aimed at it, and move toward the nearest light source. These encounters are not full boss fights; they teach the mechanics.

### Phase 2: Chase (Mid-Battle)

In the Water Treatment Plant, the ghost dashes toward Grace when provoked or when the flashlight battery is low. Turn on overhead lamps or throw flares to create safe zones. Do not sprint unless absolutely necessary, because sound attracts the ghost.

### Phase 3: Retreat (Final Battle)

The ghost teleports and attacks from behind. Keep moving, listen for audio cues, and shine light until the exit unlocks. Use the S&S M232 during stagger windows but do not waste ammo on body shots outside those windows.
