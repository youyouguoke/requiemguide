The **Titan Spinner** is a giant spider boss fought in a **ruined building in East Raccoon City** during Leon's campaign. Its armored carapace shrugs off light weapons — the fight is about hitting the **orange cluster on its abdomen**.

## Titan Spinner Overview

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Location | Ruined building, East Raccoon City |
| Chapter | 6 (guide convention) |
| Difficulty | Hard |
| HP | Very High |
| Weakness | Orange cluster on its abdomen |
| Resists | Body shots on the armored carapace |
| Recommended Weapon | Requiem magnum / explosives |

## Best Loadout for the Titan Spinner

- **Requiem magnum (Leon) or high-damage shotgun**
- **Magnum rounds and Shotgun Shells** — bring more than you think you need
- **MSBG 500 shotgun** for close-range emergencies
- **Green Herbs x 3 and First Aid Spray x 1**
- **Antidote or poison-resistant item**

## The Fight

The Titan Spinner pressures you with webs, pounces, and poison attacks while its carapace eats handgun rounds. **Circle the arena constantly**, bait its attacks, and unload on the **orange abdomen cluster** whenever it rears up. Explosive barrels in the arena give free staggers — lure it close before they blow. In the late fight, chip damage and survival matter more than aggression; don't get greedy after a stagger.

## FAQ

### What is the Titan Spinner weakness?
The orange cluster on its abdomen. The armored carapace resists most fire — focus the weak point.

### Where is the Titan Spinner fight?
A ruined building in East Raccoon City, during Leon's campaign.
