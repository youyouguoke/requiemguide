The **Blister Borne** is a grotesque, spiny mutant that Leon fights shortly after meeting Grace and Emily at the church in Chapter 2 of *Resident Evil Requiem*. Its body is covered in pulsing orange cysts that serve as its primary weak points.

This guide covers the Blister Borne location, weakness, recommended loadout, every attack pattern, phase-by-phase strategy, and tips for beating it on Standard and Hardcore difficulty.

The Blister Borne is a mobility-focused boss that rewards patience and accuracy. Unlike Chunk, who can be beaten with raw shotgun damage, the Blister Borne requires you to target specific cysts to make progress.

## Where to Find Blister Borne

The Blister Borne fight is triggered automatically on the church grounds after the cutscene where Leon, Grace, and Emily regroup. The arena is the courtyard in front of the church, with limited pillars for cover.

## Best Loadout for Blister Borne

Before the fight, make sure you have:

- **MSBG 500 shotgun** — the verified pick for popping cysts at close range
- **A healthy stock of Shotgun Shells** — cysts absorb more ammo than they look
- **S&S M232 handgun** — backup for common enemies that enter the arena
- **Green Herbs x 2** — the lunge and poison cloud deal heavy damage

## Phase-by-Phase Strategy

### Phase 1: Cyst Hunt

The Blister Borne uses Lunge and Acid Spit while cysts are exposed on its torso and limbs. Focus on one cyst at a time. Each cyst pop causes a stagger and opens a damage window. The back cyst is exposed early; the chest and stomach cysts are easiest with the shotgun.

### Phase 2: Poison Cloud

After the first cysts pop, they release poison clouds when destroyed. The Blister Borne also uses Ground Slam more frequently. Reposition after each cyst pop and never stand in the poison cloud.

### Phase 3: Final Frenzy

With most cysts destroyed, the Blister Borne lunges repeatedly and leaves acid trails. Circle the courtyard and use pillars for cover. Save your remaining heavy ammo for the last cysts and finish the fight quickly.
